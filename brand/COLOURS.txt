numoR brand kit
---------------
Colours
  Red    #C8261B   rgb(200, 38, 27)
  Black  #111111   rgb(17, 17, 17)
  White  #FFFFFF   rgb(255, 255, 255)

Typeface: Archivo (SIL Open Font License) - free for logo/commercial use.
Wordmark: Archivo, width 112. Lowercase n u m o at weight 815 scaled to cap height; R at weight 900.
For supporting text (website, documents), use Archivo Black or Archivo Bold/ExtraBold (Expanded widths match best).

Folders
  svg/wordmark        vector logo (use these wherever possible)
  svg/icon            square "n" icon; icon/transparent = the "n" alone
  svg/profile-picture square images with the full wordmark (social media)
  png/                same as svg/; wordmarks 600/1200/2400 px wide,
                      icons 32/180/192/512/1024 px, profile pictures 400/1080 px
  favicon/            ready for a website: favicon.ico, favicon.svg,
                      apple-touch-icon.png (180), icon-192.png, icon-512.png
